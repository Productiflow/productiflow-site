Once subscribed, you will receive an intake form where we collect your brand information and a link to submit projects to your queue.

You submit a request, it enters the queue, and we begin work immediately. We work through one request at a time, rotating across active clients to ensure fair turnaround for everyone. Once your eLearning module is delivered, we'll cycle back to your next queued request.

Depending on which subscription package you choose, most requests take less than 5 business days, start to finish. More complex custom-coded courses may take longer.

Revision requests are prioritized over new work and addressed in the next available production window after your feedback is received. We'll continue to revise until you're 100% satisfied.
